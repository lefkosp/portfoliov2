Performance becomes useful when it is tied to the shape of the product
experience.

## Measure The Surface

Before optimizing a component, understand what the user is waiting for. The
critical path is usually smaller than the codebase and more specific than a
framework checklist.

Good performance work makes the important path obvious.

## Keep The Model Small

Large interfaces become slow when every state change is allowed to affect every
layer. Smaller component boundaries and explicit data ownership often do more
than isolated micro-optimizations.

The goal is not clever code. The goal is predictable rendering.
